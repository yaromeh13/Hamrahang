An open-source music streaming platform that runs almost anywhere: on most web browsers, on modern and legacy smart TVs, and on Android and Android TV.

## Features

- 🎵 **Open-source music streaming**: free to use, modify, and contribute to
- 🌐 **Works in most browsers**: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, Opera, and more
- 📺 **Modern and old TVs**: optimized for both current smart TVs and older models with limited browsers
- 🤖 **Android and Android TV**: a native-feeling experience on phones, tablets, and TV boxes
